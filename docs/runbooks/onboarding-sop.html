Foundations

1Purpose, scope & TAT rules

This SOP governs onboarding in two directions. Support after go-live is governed by KTN-SOP-OPS-001.

DirectionWho is onboardedWho issues credentialsWho calls whomSection
A · Inbound (Katana provides)A merchant, branch or banker using Katana to collect or pay outKatana issues Key + Salt to the merchantMerchant calls Katana; Katana calls merchant back§4–§8
B · Outbound (Katana consumes)Katana, as platform / TSP, onto a sponsor bank, PA or TSPThe partner issues MID, client ID and secret to KatanaKatana calls partner; partner calls Katana back§9–§13

TAT rules

  • TAT is counted in business days (BD), Mon–Sat excluding bank holidays, from the day a complete document set is received. Hours are stated where used.
  • The clock pauses while Katana waits on the merchant or partner (missing document, UAT fix, unsigned agreement). Pauses are logged on the tracker with a reason.
  • Target figures are internal commitments Katana controls. Partner-dependent figures are tracked and chased, not promised to anyone.
  • A stage more than 1 BD past target is flagged in the Ops group. More than 3 BD past target goes to the Management group (§15).

4Common stage path & stage gates

All rails pass through the same eight stages. The dashboard onboarding funnel mirrors stages 2–6: APPLICATION → DOCS_PENDING → SCREENING → BANK_VERIFY → CONFIG → LIVE.

  1. Intake & rail taggingDay 0AM

    Log legal name, brand, business model, category, website / app, expected monthly TPV, transaction count and average ticket. Tag P2P INTENT PAYOUT. Reserve merchant code (e.g. UK-108). Create the lead (Leads → New lead; status APPLICATION). A merchant login with a one-time password is auto-created.

    Exit: lead exists, rails tagged, document checklist (§5) sent.

  2. Document collectionmerchant-dependent · clock not startedAM

    Status DOCS_PENDING. Chase at D+2 and D+5. A lead idle 15 BD without documents is closed and can be reopened.

    Exit: complete set received and logged; TAT clock starts.

  3. KYC / KYB & risk review1 BD · Payout 2 BDCompliance

    Status SCREENING. Verify entity (MCA / GST), directors and UBO ≥ 10 %, prohibited-category screen, sanctions & PEP screen, website / app policy pages, adverse media. Assign risk tier Low / Medium / High; High requires Management approval.

    Exit: KYC APPROVED, tier recorded. Rejection is written with reasons.

  4. Bank account & VPA verification0.5 BDCompliance + Ops

    Status BANK_VERIFY. Penny-drop or bank letter proves the settlement account is in the entity's name. For P2P, every receiving VPA is checked as a business VPA in the entity name.

    Exit: settlement account and every VPA verified.

  5. Commercials & provisioning0.5 BD · Payout 1 BDAM + Finance → Ops

    Status CONFIG. MDR / fees, settlement cycle, reserve %, limits. Anything outside the rate card needs Management. Ops sets rails, provider / branch mapping, Sub-MID (TRAFFIC or KYC_APPROVED mode), collection VPA, limits, and enables settlement.

    Exit: merchant configured; commercial terms signed.

  6. Integration & UATP2P 0.5–1 · Intent 1–3 · Payout 2–3 BDTech Integration

    Run the rail checklist (§6–§8). A witnessed live test for every enabled rail; evidence (IDs, screenshots, callback body) is attached to the tracker.

    Exit: every rail go-live checklist green; pre-go-live review passed (Intent).

  7. Go-live sign-offsame dayOps (R) · Management (A)

    Status LIVE. Ops confirms all gates; Finance co-signs Payout; Management signs High-tier merchants. Merchant added to its Telegram support groups (KTN-SOP-OPS-001 §10).

  8. Hypercare7 calendar daysOps + Finance

    Daily reconciliation check, first settlement reviewed by Finance, every incident auto-raised one severity level. Close hypercare with a one-line note on the tracker.

6.1 Prerequisites

  • Merchant exists with a merchant code; every receiving business VPA registered (primary + backups).
  • An Android phone logged into the business app (Paytm / PhonePe for Business, bank app) that receives the credit alerts. BharatPe is email-only: plan the email channel.
  • Per-trader limits configured (defaults ₹1,00,000 per transaction · ₹10,00,000 per day · 500 transactions per day; change only with Risk approval).
  • Supported on-screen RRN capture apps: Paytm for Business, PhonePe Business, Google Pay for Business. BharatPe and MobiKwik: SMS / email capture only.
  • For bankers using QR switching: QR uploaded in Banker portal, approved by admin, assigned to a store (one active QR per store, one store per QR).
  • Admin user available to trust devices.

6.3 Matching & order rules to brief the merchant on

  • Matching ladder: UTR exact → amount + payee VPA + recency → amount + recency. A unique match within 30 minutes from a trusted source auto-confirms; anything ambiguous goes to the manual reconciliation queue (Transaction Intel → Manual cases).
  • Orders expire after 15 minutes; a real credit landing after expiry auto-revives the order to SUCCESS.
  • Where two bankers share one VPA, the agent's merchant code is the only attribution key. A mistyped code orphans every capture.
  • Use clean amounts. Do not ask customers to pay odd paise amounts as a matching trick.

6.4 Go-live gate

  • Device card all four green: Trusted · Notification access · Forwarding · Online, for every phone
  • Merchant code on every device matches the dashboard exactly; Device IDs unique
  • Real test payment auto-confirmed inside 2 minutes; agent Recent activity shows a server outcome
  • Email channel ingesting (where configured): poll reports ingested ≥ 1 on the test
  • Only business VPAs registered; limits set; merchant briefed on expiry, revival and manual queue

7.3 Signing the request

Scheme 2 · HMAC-SHA256 (recommended)
  message = txnid|amount|productinfo|email
  hash    = HMAC_SHA256(key = KEY + SALT, message)                    → lowercase hex, 64 chars

Scheme 1 · SHA-512
  seq  = key|txnid|amount|productinfo|firstname|email|||||||||||salt   (11 pipes between email and salt)
  hash = SHA512(seq)                                                  → lowercase hex, 128 chars

Omitted fields sign as empty strings in position. Avoid | ~ = in txnid and productinfo.

7.9 Go-live gate

  • Production Key + Salt issued; Salt server-side only (not browser, app binary or Git); scheme confirmed
  • Collection VPA verified on a real payment; txnid prefixed with merchant code; reused checked
  • Callback handler verifies HASH, compares AMOUNT numerically, is idempotent on ORDER_ID, replies 2xx < 8 s
  • Fulfilment only on server-side confirmation; browser-return parameters treated as display only
  • Recon sweep scheduled; EXPIRED re-checked; UAT I-1 to I-8 passed with evidence on tracker
  • Merchant with its own acquirer MID: their Key + Salt vaulted in Katana; webhook https://katanapay.co/api/gateway/payu/webhook (Payments · Successful + Failed · POST) added in their gateway dashboard; test callback shows hash_verified: true. 8.3 Payout lifecycle & operator desk

Below threshold → QUEUED to the FIFO operator queue (assign ≤ 2 min, accept ≤ 3 min). At or above threshold → HOLD pending second approval. Operator accepts → processes → completes with UTR / tx hash and proof, or rejects / holds with reason.

Settlement (KTN-SET-000001):
REQUESTED → ACCEPTED → PROCESSING → PAID | USDT_TRANSFERRED → UTR_SUBMITTED → VERIFIED → RECONCILED
exceptions: ON_HOLD · CORRECTION_REQUIRED · INVALID_BENEFICIARY · INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE · ESCALATED · FAILED · REVERSED

8.5 Go-live gate

  • Limits, threshold, modes and networks recorded and signed by Finance

13.1 Direction A field mapping

When Katana consumes a partner API, record these on the partner config before writing code. Katana signs outbound calls with the partner's secret and receives notifications on a callback URL issued at onboarding.

Where it is configured in Katana. A PA for UPI Intent is set once per provider: PUT /api/providers/{id}/integrations (Super Admin) with base_url, pay_id, client_id, return_url, callback_url, env (SANDBOX / PROD). The secret and API key go to the vault (AES-256-GCM). PROD cannot be enabled without base URL + secret, and the config applies to every branch mapped to that provider. A single merchant can override it via PATCH /api/merchants/{id}/payment-config (poolpay.enabled, env, pay_id, settlement_vpa(s) ≤ 20). An own PayU MID goes in via POST /api/merchants/{id}/gateway-mid.

Build flag. Katana signs with HMAC / SHA-256 / SHA-512 only. A partner that mandates RSA payload encryption, mTLS client certificates or a status-enquiry API not yet mapped needs a Tech build estimate before the agreement stage. Add it to the partner TAT at that point, not after UAT starts.
