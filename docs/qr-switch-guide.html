Katana Payment Orchestrator

Static QR Switch

How a banker moves a merchant store from one QR endpoint to another — immediately, once, and with a record that cannot be edited afterwards.

AudienceKatana Admin · Banker
Applies tokatanapay.co
VersionPhase 1 · Aug 2026

1What this solves

Until now, the QR a store collects on existed only inside the banker's payment app. Changing it meant someone quietly swapping a printed code — no record of who changed what, when, or why, and no way to see which endpoint a store is actually live on.

The QR Switch module makes that one action a first-class operation in Katana. A banker keeps a pool of its own QR endpoints, each store is allocated exactly one of them, and moving a store between them is a single click that is recorded, auditable and safe to retry.

Katana Admin

Sets it up

Creates merchant stores, approves the QRs bankers submit, and allocates each store its first endpoint.

Banker

Runs it day to day

Adds its own QRs, and switches any store it serves onto a different one of its QRs when something goes wrong.

2Before you start

You needWhere it comes from
A MerchantAn existing record under Merchants in the admin sidebar. Stores are created underneath one.
A banker codeThe code of the banker that will collect the money — e.g. PRIMESX. Visible under Banker in the admin sidebar.
At least two UPI IDsFor that same banker. One goes live on the store; the second is what the banker switches to. See section 4.

3Admin — one-time setup

Everything in this section happens on one screen: QR Operations, in the admin sidebar under Payment Management.

katanapay.co/qr-switch
  1. Create the store
    Stores tab → New store
    Choose the Merchant it belongs to, then give it a store code (for example S001) and a name such as “Mumbai Store 01”. City is optional. Store codes only need to be unique within that merchant.
  2. Add the banker's first QR
    QR inventory tab → Add QR
    Enter the banker code, the UPI ID the QR resolves to, and which payment app it belongs to (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, MobiKwik or Other). Settlement type, daily limit and a QR image are optional. A QR you add as admin is approved immediately.
  3. Add a second QR for the same banker
    QR inventory tab → Add QR
    This is the step most people skip. Without a spare, the banker will have nothing to switch to.
  4. Allocate the first endpoint
    Stores tab → Allocate QR
    Pick one of the approved, free QRs. Whoever owns that QR becomes this store's banker, and the store starts collecting on it.
Approving what bankers submit

Bankers can add their own QRs from their portal. Those arrive as PENDING and appear at the top of your QR inventory list with Approve and Reject buttons. A QR cannot be switched to until it is approved.

4Banker — switching a store's QR

The banker signs in to its own portal and uses QR switch, the second item in the sidebar.

katanapay.co/banker-portal/qr-switch
  1. Check the three counters at the top “Stores you collect for”, “QRs free to switch to”, and “Awaiting approval”. If the middle one reads 0, no switch is possible yet — add a QR and have it approved first.
  2. Find the store The table lists only stores your QRs are currently live on, with the merchant name, store code, and the exact UPI ID that store is collecting on right now.
  3. Click Switch QR A confirmation opens showing the endpoint the store is leaving, spelled out in full.
  4. Pick the replacement and confirm Choose from your free approved QRs, optionally record a reason (“not scanning”, “limit reached”, “settlement delayed”), then press Switch now.
  5. Confirm it took The row updates immediately to the new UPI ID. The QR you left returns to your pool, so you can switch back to it at any time.
The rule that trips everyone up

Switch QR stays greyed out until that banker has a second approved, unallocated QR. One QR means there is nothing to switch to. Hover the disabled button and it tells you exactly that. Adding a QR is not enough on its own — an admin still has to approve it.

Adding your own QRs

On the same screen, Add QR puts a new UPI ID into your pool. It sits as PENDING until a Katana admin approves it, then becomes available to switch to.

5What the system will not let you do

Each of these refusals exists because the alternative silently misroutes real money. They fail loudly, with the reason on screen.

If you try to…You will see
Switch onto another banker's QR “Target QR belongs to another banker.” Cross-banker failover is a later phase; it is blocked for admins too, not just bankers.
Switch a store you don't serve “This store is currently served by another banker.”
Switch to a pending or rejected QR “Target QR is pending, not approved.”
Switch to a QR already live on another store It will not appear in the list at all. One QR serves one store at a time.
Switch to the QR the store is already on “Store is already on this QR.”
Register a UPI ID that already exists “That UPI ID is already registered on another QR” — including under a different banker.
Reject or pause a QR that is live “Switch the store away before rejecting it.”
Double-clicking is safe

Every switch carries an idempotency key. A double-click, a retried request or a refreshed page returns the original switch rather than performing a second one — you will see “Already switched — nothing changed”. If two people switch two different stores onto the same free QR at the same instant, exactly one succeeds and the other is told the QR was just taken.

6Status reference

A QR carries two independent statuses. They answer different questions, and one does not imply the other.

Approval status — may this QR be used at all?

StatusMeaning
PENDINGA banker submitted it. Not usable until an admin approves it.
APPROVEDCleared for use. Eligible to be switched to, if it is also free.
REJECTEDRefused by an admin, with a reason. Its UPI ID can be submitted again later.

Routing status — what is this QR doing right now?

StatusMeaning
AVAILABLEFree. This is what a switch candidate looks like.
ALLOCATEDLive on a store and collecting.
PAUSEDTaken out of the switch pool by an admin without withdrawing approval.
Why two statuses

Approval is about eligibility; routing is about operation. Pausing an approved QR removes it from switch candidates without re-opening the approval question, and un-pausing does not require re-approval.

7Troubleshooting

SymptomCauseFix
Switch QR button is greyed out No second approved, free QR for this banker Add a QR, then have an admin approve it
Banker's store list is empty No store has been allocated to this banker yet Admin allocates the first QR (section 3, step 4)
New QR does not appear as a choice Still PENDING, or paused, or live on another store Check its two statuses in QR inventory
“UPI ID is already registered” That VPA exists on a non-rejected QR Search QR inventory for it; reject the stale one first
Cannot reject or pause a QR It is currently live on a store Switch that store elsewhere, then retry
QR image will not upload Wrong file type or over 8 MB PNG, JPEG or WEBP only. The image is optional — the QR record works without it

8Not in this phase

Stated plainly so nobody plans around something that is not there yet.

CapabilityStatus
Cross-banker failover Not built. A store can only move between QRs of the banker already serving it.
Approval workflow for switches Not built by design — a banker's click executes immediately. Every switch is fully audited, but no admin gate stands in front of it.
Multiple live QRs per store Not honoured. Exactly one endpoint per store is enforced.
Automatic switching on incidents Not built. Incident capture, health scoring and automatic replacement selection are later phases of the BRD.
Merchant-facing notification Not built. The merchant is not told when its store's endpoint changes.
Important

A switch changes where money lands. It takes effect the moment it is confirmed, with no approval step and no undo prompt — switching back is itself another switch. Record a reason: it is the only context anyone reviewing the history will have.
