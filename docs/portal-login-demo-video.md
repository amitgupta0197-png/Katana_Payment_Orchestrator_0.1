# Katana Portals — Merchant & Banker Login (Production Video Script)

**Format:** 16:9 landscape (1920×1080, desktop screen-recording) · **Length:** ~2:50 (90s short cut below) · **Voiceover:** Hinglish · **Captions:** English (burned-in) · **Music:** calm corporate, ~12%.

**Goal:** Show a new merchant and a new banker exactly how they sign in, what they land on, and why they can only ever see their own data.

**The one big idea to land:** *there is only ONE login page for everybody.* No separate merchant URL, no separate banker URL. Katana reads your **role** from your account and sends you to the right portal automatically. Say this twice — it is the single most common onboarding question.

## Recording checklist

**⚠️ Do not record against live production data.** Both dashboards render real merchant balances, real payer data, and real banker capital positions. Record against a local dev instance (`pnpm dev` → `localhost:3100`) with seeded demo data, or a purpose-made demo merchant + demo banker. If you must use katanapay.co, blur every amount in post.

**⚠️ The `demo` password no longer works in production.** The demo-login fallback is disabled when `NODE_ENV=production` (security audit C5). On prod you need a real account with a real password. Locally it still works, which is another reason to record locally.

Before rolling:
- [ ] Browser at 1920×1080, zoom 100%, bookmarks bar hidden, one clean tab.
- [ ] **Disable Chrome autofill / use a guest profile.** On a normal profile the login form
      autofills a saved real credential, which will end up in your footage.
- [ ] Scene 12 mentions the 20% amber threshold — the seed is green at 42.9%, so either keep
      the VO as-is (describing the rule) or switch the seed and show it.

## Assembly
1. **Record:** one continuous take per part in **Screen Studio** (Mac) — its auto zoom-on-click does most of the polish for free. CapCut is the free alternative.
2. **Voiceover:** paste the Hinglish read into **ElevenLabs** with a warm Indian-English voice → MP3 → lay under the footage. Hinglish reads best on a voice tuned for Indian English, not Hindi — the English nouns (dashboard, settlement, commission) stay clean.
3. **Captions:** CapCut auto-captions, then replace with the English caption column — they are deliberately shorter than the VO so they don't fight it.
4. **Highlights:** a soft rounded-rectangle callout on each KPI tile as it's named. Don't animate more than one at a time.
5. **Export:** 1080p MP4, H.264, target under 25 MB.

| Wrong portal is blocked instantly | `src/middleware.ts` — edge persona partition + `homeFor()` bounce |

| One-time password → change it in Profile | `src/app/{merchant,banker}-portal/profile/page.tsx` → `POST /api/me/password` |

**Not mentioned in the VO on purpose** (true, but too much for this audience): the session cookie is HMAC-signed with an 8-hour life and is revocable via a per-user epoch; MFA prompts for a 6-digit code only if the account has it enrolled; 8 failed sign-ins in 15 minutes locks the account (`LOGIN_LOCK_THRESHOLD` / `LOGIN_LOCK_WINDOW_MIN`). If you want a security-focused cut for partners, these are the beats to add.
